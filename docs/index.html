Passway support

Passway lets adults connect after meeting in person, using a short lived pairing code, and chat without displaying their sign in phone numbers to each other.

For help, email Limerencetechnologies@gmail.com. Describe the problem and the app version if available. Do not send passwords, one time codes, bank details or unnecessary chat content.

Your information and account

Read the privacy notice for information about data processing, providers and retention. See account deletion to delete an account in the app or request deletion without reinstalling it.

Safety

You can decline a connection, end a chat, block another account or report a conversation from the app. Do not share money or sensitive information with a new contact. Passway is not an emergency service. If there is an immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

This support website does not establish whether an app release has been approved or is publicly available on Google Play.
