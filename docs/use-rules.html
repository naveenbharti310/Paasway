Passway use rules

Version 2026-10-17.1. These rules apply to connecting and sending content through Passway. Accept them in the app before connecting or sending content. You may decline and sign out or delete your account without accepting.

  1. Passway is for adults aged 18 and over. Connect only with people you have met in person who want to connect with you.
  2. Do not send threats, harassment, bullying, hate, discriminatory abuse, sexually explicit content or non-consensual sexual content.
  3. Never create, request, share or promote child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, trafficking, or any sexual content involving minors.
  4. Do not impersonate people, scam others, request fraudulent payments, send spam, distribute malware, or promote unlawful activity.
  5. Respect privacy. Do not share another person’s private information without permission. Do not evade a block or an account restriction.
  6. Use the chat menu to report a conversation, block someone or end a connection. Reports close the chat and block that account. Passway may restrict accounts and preserve safety evidence as explained in the privacy notice.
  7. Automated screening is not a guarantee that content is safe. Support is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services if there is immediate danger. For other safety concerns, contact Limerencetechnologies@gmail.com. Do not email illegal images or videos.

These rules do not replace the privacy notice. For deletion options, see Delete account.
