Delete your Passway account

You can delete your Passway account in the app, or request deletion by email if you no longer have app access.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Passway and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the You tab to reach Settings.
  3. In the Account section, choose Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation and choose Delete account again.

A deletion request starts a retryable server operation. It can remain in progress if a service is unavailable. Signing out or uninstalling the app alone does not delete the account.

Request deletion without the app

Email Limerencetechnologies@gmail.com with the subject Passway account deletion. State that you want your Passway account and associated data deleted and include the phone number associated with that account so support can locate it. Do not include a password, SMS code, bank details or unnecessary chat content. Support may need to verify that you own the account before deleting it. If you no longer control that phone number, explain that in the request instead of asking someone else for a code.

What is removed

Deletion removes the Firebase sign in account and profile, ends open chats, cancels pending pairing activity and removes your private notes. The app prevents new account activity while deletion is in progress.

What may remain

Messages already sent remain subject to their normal 72 hour visibility period and asynchronous cleanup. Reported conversations and reports can be retained for a 90 day safety window or an existing longer deadline; later reports can extend it. Reported phone safety holds can remain for 180 days. Banned phone holds and block records have no automatic expiry. A minimal old account identifier and phone hash tombstone can remain for seven days after deletion completes to prevent cached credentials recreating the deleted profile. Service provider operational records may follow their own retention rules.

These safety exceptions mean deletion is not a promise that every record disappears immediately. Further details are in the privacy notice. For a question about retained information or a request concerning particular data, contact the support address above.
